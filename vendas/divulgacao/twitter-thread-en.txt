Tweet 4/7
The killer feature: langcheck.

Whisper hallucinates languages on hard audio (Arabic, Mandarin, Japanese).

`langcheck` re-decodes each cue, compares detected vs expected language, flags hallucinations.

Caught a forced-French pass "inventing" French over Hassaniya Arabic.

Tweet 5/7
Translation stays text-only.

Batches of 30 cues -> plain text files.

You translate the text, the CLI rebuilds the SRT preserving timing.

Zero risk of desync. 3x faster than cue-by-cue.
