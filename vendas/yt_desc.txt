Subtitle a whole film in under 8 hours — fully local, free, no API cost.

SubFlow is a 100% local subtitle pipeline: transcribe with Whisper, translate in batches, clean, QC (Netflix-style: max 42 chars/line, CPS limits, no overlaps) and language-check for ASR hallucinations.

Pipeline:
- subflow run video --src-lang fr --tgt-lang pt
- subflow clean + qc (automatic fixes)
- subflow langcheck (catches Whisper hallucination)

Real case: a 104-min documentary (Med Hondo, 1977) subtitled in 7h50 — half the time of manual work.
